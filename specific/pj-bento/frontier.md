## 3. Two-Week Study Cycle

### Regular Week: 21 Blocks

| Work | Blocks | Time |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Weakest section | 9 | 3h |
| Second-weakest section | 5 | 1h 40m |
| Strongest section | 3 | 1h |
| Error replay and mixed retrieval | 4 | 1h 20m |

### Simulation Week: 21 Blocks

| Work | Blocks | Time |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Uninterrupted full-format simulation | 6 | 2h |
| Review every wrong or uncertain item | 6 | 2h |
| Primary bottleneck remediation | 5 | 1h 40m |
| Secondary bottleneck remediation | 3 | 1h |
| Spaced retrieval of recurring rules | 1 | 20m |

After the simulation, rank sections by accuracy. Break ties using low-confidence count, then time pressure. Reallocate the next cycle from that ranking.

### Default 20-Minute Block

1. `0:00–2:00` — retrieve the last two relevant error rules without notes.
2. `2:00–12:00` — answer 5–8 questions from the target type.
3. `12:00–17:00` — identify the decisive evidence and eliminate every distractor.
4. `17:00–20:00` — retry uncertain items and update the ledger.

For listening, do not expose the transcript before the first answer. Text-only dialogues count as reading, not listening.

## 4. Error Ledger

Allowed causes:

- `language` — vocabulary, grammar, collocation, or kanji
- `relationship` — status, distance, role, or register
- `intent` — request, refusal, apology, proposal, concern, or implication
- `information` — person, number, time, condition, exception, or next action
- `integration` — failure to reconcile audio with a chart, schedule, email, or document
- `timing` — comprehension was correct but too slow
- `strategy` — over-inference, premature answering, or changing an evidence-backed answer

Promote a lesson into [`reference.md`](reference.md) only after it recurs or clearly generalizes across question types.

## 5. Booking Gate

Book the real test only after two **fresh** full-format simulations, completed within three weeks, both meet every condition:

- overall raw accuracy `>= 75%`;
- each section accuracy `>= 65%`;
- low-confidence correct rate `<= 15%`;
- all sections completed within their time limits;
- no error rule recurs three or more times across the two simulations.

These are conservative internal heuristics, not a published conversion to BJT 400. Free materials provide less certainty than an official full mock, so the gate deliberately requires consistency and a section floor.
