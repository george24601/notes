# PJ Bento

PJ Bento is a readiness-based BJT preparation project with one north-star metric:

> **Earn an official BJT score of at least 400.**

## Why 400

The purpose of this target is concrete:

- A BJT score of **400 or higher earns 10 points** under Japan's Highly Skilled Professional (`高度人材ポイント制`) system, where it is treated as equivalent to JLPT N2.
- BJT is directly relevant to processing the meetings, documents, relationships, and implied actions encountered while working at a Japanese internet company.

The immigration threshold and the BJT's internal bands are different systems. A score of 400 is within BJT J3 (`320–419`), while BJT J2 starts at 420; **420 is not required for the 10 immigration points**. A score of 480 or higher earns 15 points, but that is outside the current project scope.

Official references, verified on 2026-09-22:

- [Immigration Services Agency: Highly Skilled Professional points Q&A](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/newimmiact_3_qa.html)
- [Official BJT: benefits and immigration recognition](https://www.kanken.or.jp/bjt/about/feature_merit.html)

## Metric Hierarchy

1. **Outcome metric:** official BJT score `>= 400`.
2. **Booking signals:** fresh-practice accuracy, section floor, confidence, pacing, and error recurrence.
3. **Secondary benefit:** improved comprehension and response quality at work.

Practice percentages are operational signals only. BJT uses IRT-based scaled scoring, so raw accuracy must never be converted into a predicted BJT score.

## Exam Shape

| Section | Time | Question types | Questions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | ---: |
| Listening | About 45 min | Situational, utterance, and general listening comprehension | 25 |
| Listening + Reading | About 30 min | Situation, document, and integrated listening-reading comprehension | 25 |
| Reading | 30 min | Vocabulary/grammar, expression, and general reading comprehension | 30 |

The test contains 80 questions and takes about two hours including instructions and a questionnaire. The score report contains the overall 0–800 score and a rank from 1–7 for each section.

## Operating Rules

- Study seven hours per week in 20-minute units.
- Reallocate time every two weeks toward the weakest measured section.
- Use one uninterrupted two-hour simulation every second week.
- Review wrong answers and low-confidence correct answers with equal seriousness.
- Explain why the best option fits and why every distractor fails.
- Count listening practice only when audio is heard without seeing the transcript first.
- Use free official materials; generated items are remediation tools, not official-score predictors.
- Book the test only after the readiness gate in [`frontier.md`](frontier.md) is met.
