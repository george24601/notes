# BJT Error-Repair Reference

Use this file only after a question exposes a gap. It is not a syllabus and should not be studied from top to bottom.

## 1. Decision Cues by Section

| Section | Extract first | Typical trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Listening | speaker roles, purpose, constraint, and next action | choosing grammatical language that is socially wrong |
| Listening + Reading | question target, visual axes, changed value, and exception | processing the audio and document separately |
| Reading | document purpose, actor, deadline, condition, and exception | translating every word and losing the decision point |

Always separate the literal statement from the business action it implies.

### Nine Question Types

- `場面把握`: Identify the visible situation and the description that matches it exactly.
- `発言聴解`: Infer what is appropriate for this speaker, listener, and moment.
- `総合聴解`: Track changes, reasons, constraints, and the final decision across a longer exchange.
- `状況把握`: Reconcile the spoken instruction with the displayed situation.
- `資料聴読解`: Locate the field in the document that the audio changes or qualifies.
- `総合聴読解`: Integrate multiple details; note contradictions and exceptions.
- `語彙・文法`: Choose the form that fits both grammar and business relationship.
- `表現読解`: Identify the expression appropriate to the document's purpose and audience.
- `総合読解`: Find the main purpose, responsible actor, required action, and deadline.

## 2. Known Risks from Earlier Practice

Treat these as hypotheses until a BJT-format error confirms them.

### Particles and Roles

| Risk | Correct pattern | Decision effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `原因を分かっていません` | `原因が分かっていません` | `分かる` takes `が` |
| `設定は原因の可能性があります` | `設定が原因の可能性があります` | Marks the specific possible cause |
| `この内容が誰が担当ですか` | `この内容は誰が担当ですか` | Separates topic from responsible actor |
| `誰が聞けばいいですか` | `誰に聞けばいいですか` | `に` identifies the target of asking |
| `技術戦略則り` | `技術戦略に則り` | Preserve the fixed particle |

### Sequence, Condition, and Voice

| Risk | Correct pattern | Decision effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `終わってたら連絡します` | `終わったら連絡します` | Contact happens when completion occurs |
| `確認してたら返事します` | `確認してから返事します` | Confirmation must happen before reply |
| `問題が見つけていません` | `問題は見つかっていません` | Uses the intransitive result state |
| `相談していいですか` | `相談してもいいですか` | Complete permission pattern |
| `相談さしてください` | `相談させてください` | Correct causative request |

### Comprehension Pressure

- Long clauses may hide the decisive fact at the end.
- A negative-looking form such as `少ない` may describe a small amount rather than negate an action.
- Parallel reasons often appear as `〜ことと、〜ことです`.
- Formal connectors can carry the rule: `〜にかかわらず`, `〜のうえ`, `〜べき`, `〜であったため`.
- Numbers, changed schedules, exceptions, and who owns the next action deserve notes; decorative detail does not.

## 3. High-Yield Contrasts

### Time and Sequence

- `まで` = until; `までに` = by a deadline.
- `〜てから` = after intentionally doing X; `〜たら` = when/once X occurs.
- `〜のうえ` / `〜た上で` = after a required review or prerequisite.
- `〜次第` = promptly once something occurs; recognize it even if it is not George's preferred speaking style.

### Cause, Possibility, and Risk

- `〜ので` / `〜ため` state cause; `〜であったため` is common in formal reports.
- `かもしれません` is softer possibility.
- `可能性があります` is analytical possibility.
- `恐れがあります` flags an undesirable risk.

### Relationship and Register

- `〜てもいいですか` asks permission directly.
- `〜ていただけますか` asks the listener to act politely.
- `〜させてください` asks permission for the speaker to act.
- `〜いたします` is humble and can be the correct client-facing choice.
- `〜させていただきます` may be appropriate when permission or benefit is genuinely involved; do not reject it merely because it is ceremonious.
- Judge honorific language from speaker, listener, direction of benefit, and business setting—not length alone.

### Conditions and Scope

- `〜にかかわらず` = regardless of the condition.
- `〜なしで` = without a required item or approval.
- `〜の中でも` highlights one item within a set.
- `〜べき` expresses an expected or required action.
- `もしくは` gives a formal alternative.

### Actor and Result

- `は` sets the topic; `が` marks the specific subject or new information.
- `見つける` is transitive; `見つかる` describes something being found.
- A plain-form clause can modify a noun directly: `共有する予定の対象テーブル`.
- Embedded questions use `〜か`: `誰が担当するか確認します`.

## 4. Business Intent Cues

| Intent | Common cues | What to identify |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Confirmation | `〜という理解で合っていますか` | proposition being confirmed |
| Request | `〜ていただけますか` | requested actor and action |
| Permission | `〜てもよろしいでしょうか` | action the speaker wants to take |
| Proposal | `〜がよいと思います` | recommended option and reason |
| Concern | `〜恐れがあります` | threatened outcome and condition |
| Deferral | `確認してから返事します` | prerequisite and later action |
| Commitment | `〜までに実施します` | owner, action, and deadline |
| Escalation | `上長に相談します` | unresolved decision and next owner |
| Exception | `〜にかかわらず` / `ただし` | rule boundary or override |

## 5. Review Procedure

For every wrong or low-confidence answer:

1. State the speaker's or document's goal in one short Japanese sentence.
2. Cite the exact word, number, relationship, or visual field that decides the answer.
3. Explain why each distractor fails: language, relationship, intent, or fact.
4. Write one reusable rule of no more than one sentence.
5. Re-answer the item later without seeing the explanation.

Add new material here only when it is reusable across situations. Keep one-off vocabulary in the live error ledger until it recurs.
